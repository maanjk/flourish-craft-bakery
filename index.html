✦ Our Story

Baked with patience,
served with joy

Flourish Craft Bakery started as a tiny family kitchen with one stubborn belief — that great bread takes time. From our stone-baked sourdough to our 27-layer croissants, everything is made from scratch with premium butter, unbleached flour and a whole lot of love.

Tucked into Deans Arcade, I-16 Markaz, we've grown into the neighbourhood's favourite morning ritual. No premixes, no shortcuts, no rushing the dough.

✦ Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our sourdough, custom cake lead times, or delivery areas? We've got you covered.

🥖 How should I store and reheat my sourdough bread?

+
Store your loaf cut-side down on a wooden cutting board or inside a paper/cloth bread bag at room temperature (avoid the fridge as it stales bread faster!). To revive that freshly baked crackling crust, preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F), splash or mist the loaf lightly with a little water, and bake for 5 to 7 minutes. It will emerge just like it left our stone oven!

🎂 How far in advance do I need to order a custom cake?

+
For signature celebration cakes (Naked Cake, Belgian Fudge, Red Velvet), we ask for 24 to 48 hours notice. For bespoke fondant, multi-tiered wedding, or intricate sculpted theme cakes, please place your order 3 to 5 days in advance so our cake artists have ample time to craft your custom details.

🛵 Do you deliver across Islamabad & Rawalpindi?

+
Yes! We offer direct boutique delivery across Islamabad (including I, H, G, F, E sectors, Bahria Town, DHA) and central Rawalpindi via dedicated couriers. All orders are carefully packed in insulated, sturdy bakery boxes to ensure your bakes and cakes arrive in pristine condition.

💳 What payment methods do you accept?

+
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) for Islamabad/Rawalpindi orders, direct Raast Instant Pay, Bank Transfer, JazzCash, and EasyPaisa. You can select your preferred method upon confirming your order via WhatsApp.

🌱 Are there vegetarian or eggless options available?

+
Yes! All of our artisan sourdough breads, baguettes, and focaccia are 100% naturally vegan (flour, water, wild yeast starter, and salt). We also bake eggless tea cakes, vegan cookies, and eggless custom cake sponges on request — just look for the "Veg" badge or mention it in your order notes!
